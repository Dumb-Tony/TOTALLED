# Crash Lab 0.7

Play: https://dumb-tony.github.io/TOTALLED/

This pass improves the sedan as a complete object and gives driving/crashes more feedback.

- Painted window surrounds, rubber seals and wider rear pillars close gaps around the cabin. Glass disappears permanently when its own frame distorts severely.
- Wider shaped flanks, crowned hood/trunk/roof, deeper wheel tubs and arch lips, recessed steel wheels, bumper depth and dark panel undersides. Detached doors reveal cabin openings.
- Cleaner paint with restrained edge wear; deformed panels keep their paint color with rougher/scuffed shading. Brake lenses brighten under service braking.
- Smooth steering rack motion, speed-sensitive maximum lock, approximate Ackermann geometry and a shared tire friction limit for acceleration/braking/cornering. Damaged mounting geometry still controls tire directions.
- Opposite throttle brakes a moving car before engaging reverse. Rear handbrake remains separate from the four-wheel brake.
- Original synthesized engine, impact and tire sounds. Sliding tires leave visible marks. Press M to mute.

Limits: geometric detail remains procedural prototype art. Glass loss is cosmetic, without simulated shards; wheel tubs, bumpers, engine and interior skin to structural nodes rather than having separate collision bodies. Steering geometry uses nominal wheelbase/track, and tire forces remain approximate, without angular tire dynamics or a calibrated load/slip tire model. Tire marks are bounded to the most recent 256 segments per car; physical wrecks and debris retain their existing persistence. No self-collision, multiplayer or AI is added.
